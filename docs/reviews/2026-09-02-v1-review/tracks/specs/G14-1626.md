## Problem

The issue asks for two things. Part 1: reuse `renderMarkdown` in `CommentThread.svelte`,
`CommentCard.svelte` and `SuggestionCard.svelte`'s reason. Part 2: carry `suggestedText` on a reply.

**Measurement: part 1 already shipped, and the issue's anchors for it are stale.** It landed in
PR #1636 (`df8c62f0`, merged `b9e3726d`), which introduced `src/client/panels/AnnotationBody.svelte`
and routed all three targets through it. Verified on `33a99d00`:

| `SuggestionCard.svelte:97-101` — reason, plain | `:98` — `<AnnotationBody …>` |

The issue's two "settle rather than assume" items are settled too, in that PR:

- **Whose text renders as markdown.** `AnnotationBody.svelte:47`'s `asMarkdown = author === "claude"`
  matches the chat gate, with a docblock (`chat-markdown.ts:31-38`) stating it is a *semantic* choice
  and explicitly not a trust boundary. Pinned by four specs in
  `tests/client/annotation-body.test.ts:18-55` (claude formats; user, import and unknown-author
  render literally) and by the second E2E case in `tests/e2e/annotation-markdown.spec.ts:130-153`.
  **This is the run's required "whose text renders as markdown" decision** — inherited from #1636,
  re-verified here rather than taken on faith, and carried as the first entry in `G14-1639.md`'s
  `## Assumptions` block so the run's journal shows it was made rather than skipped.
- **Escaping tests before the surfaces multiply.** They exist:
  `describe("renderMarkdown — the {@html} escaping invariant")` at
  `tests/client/chat-markdown.test.ts:263` asserts scheme allowlisting with a pinned `^` anchor,
  href-breakout, the label/URL DoS bounds, and an attribute **allowlist** over the parsed DOM.

## Not in scope

**Part 2 — `suggestedText` on replies — is not started.** It touches the wire, the durable envelope
and the accept path; the issue itself says the "what does accept mean on a reply" question is a
design decision to settle before code; and the owner's 2026-08-26 comment gates it behind #1629
(the accept path did not verify target text while undo did). Nothing is added to `AnnotationReply`,
to `AnnotationReplyRecordV1`, or to `tandem_annotationReply`.

**One residual part-1 sub-question, undecided and deliberately left so — for Bryan.** The issue's
"Proposed" section also asks what `tandem_exportAnnotations` and the `.docx` Word-comment write-back
should do with markdown now living in an annotation body: emit the raw `**markers**`, or convert.
#1636 did not answer it. Measured, not assumed: no module under `src/server/` imports
`renderMarkdown` or any markdown-stripping helper (zero hits for
`renderMarkdown|markdownToText|stripMarkdown` under `src/server/`), so both writers emit the stored
text verbatim and a Word comment shows literal asterisks. That is a product call about what leaves
the app in a user's file, not a client rendering bug, so it is not decided here — and it is why
#1626 stays open even setting part 2 aside. **Per Done-when 1 it is filed as a comment on #1626
before the PR body references it.**
